# ClearMind Medical

Patient onboarding for a virtual psychiatry practice.

## The key insight

We are building for a **neurodiverse population**, so the workflow has to be
minimal and clutter-free. Every screen is designed to reduce cognitive load and
remove distraction. What someone has to hold in their head at once is the
constraint everything else bends around.

## What that produced

- **Five steps instead of eight**, with few words on each.
- **Short taps by default** — one optional free-text question, and a microphone
  if talking is easier than typing.
- **Two clinicians to choose between, not a directory.** Choice overload is the
  failure mode.
- **Nothing is ever forced.** Any question can be skipped, and the flow moves
  on. No loops, no nagging.
- **No password**, and the appointment is held while details are filled in, so
  nothing is a race.

Onboarding finishes at the first visit rather than the payment, so a
returning-patient portal carries visits, messaging and medications.

## Status

A prototype. Clinicians, appointments and records are fictional; identity,
insurance and payment are simulated.
